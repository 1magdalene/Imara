A simple security checklist for growing Kenyan teams

Imara IT Team

Business technology specialists
Oct 26, 2026 • 18 Comments

Security does not have to begin with a large budget or a complicated security platform. For many Kenyan SMEs, the biggest improvements come from getting the basics consistent across people, devices, and accounts.

Start with the controls your team can maintain. Review who has access, keep business devices updated, and make sure important files are backed up somewhere you can actually restore from.

Start with identity and access

Most incidents become harder to contain when former staff, shared passwords, or unnecessary administrator accounts remain active. Build a simple access routine:

  • Use individual accounts instead of shared passwords
  • Turn on multi-factor authentication where available
  • Remove access promptly when someone leaves
  • Review administrator accounts every quarter

Three controls worth prioritising

  • Multi-factor authentication for email and cloud tools
  • Automatic updates and endpoint protection
  • Tested backups for critical business files

Protect the devices people use every day

A secure policy is not enough if laptops, routers, and servers are left unpatched. Keep a current device list, schedule updates, and use a consistent baseline for antivirus, firewall, and screen-lock settings.

"Strong security is built from repeatable habits, clear ownership, and backups that have been tested before they are needed."

Imara IT Solutions, Nairobi

Make ownership clear

Assign someone to review alerts, updates, access, and backups so important tasks do not disappear between teams.

Plan for recovery

Know which systems matter most, where their backups are stored, and how quickly your team needs them restored.
