About Us

Strong IT for strong businesses

“Imara” is Swahili for strong, and that’s the standard we hold ourselves to. Imara IT Solutions was founded in Nairobi to help small and medium businesses get dependable technology without the cost of a full in-house IT department.

Whether you run a clinic, a school, a law firm, or a growing retail chain, we take care of your devices, networks, and user accounts, and we explain everything in plain language. No jargon, no surprise bills.

  • Reliability: We show up, and we fix things properly the first time.
  • Clarity: We communicate clearly and transparently about our services and pricing.
  • Expertise: Our team has the knowledge and experience to handle any IT challenge.

Mission: Our mission is to provide reliable, affordable, and secure IT solutions to every small and medium business in Kenya.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about Imara IT Solutions.

Do I need a contract?

No. Our plans are month-to-month, and you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel with 30 days' notice.

How fast will you respond to a problem?

Within 30 minutes during business hours. Business and Enterprise clients also get 24/7 emergency support.

Can you support us if we already have an IT person?

Yes. We can work alongside your existing staff, covering after-hours support, security, or specialist projects.

What happens if we lose our data?

We restore your systems from clean backups and help close the gap that caused the problem.

Do you work outside Nairobi?

We offer remote support across Kenya and on-site visits in Nairobi and surrounding areas.
